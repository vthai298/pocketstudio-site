PocketStudio

A private production tool for 2D animated YouTube channels.

What it does

PocketStudio helps a small studio write, voice and animate original 2D videos (stories for kids, science explainers, everyday-economics stories and retellings of public-domain books). Every video goes through human review gates (idea, script, finished video) before it is uploaded.

How it uses YouTube

  • A channel owner connects their YouTube channel once through Google's own consent screen. PocketStudio never sees or stores Google passwords.
  • After a video is approved, PocketStudio uploads it to that channel as private, sets its title, description, tags, thumbnail and audience (videos for kids are always marked "made for kids"), and schedules the public release at least 24 hours later.
  • It can update the channel's own description and banner when the owner asks.
  • The channel owner can disconnect at any time.

PocketStudio uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's use of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Who can use it

PocketStudio is not a public service. It is used by the studio for its own channels and for channels of partners who have agreed to work with the studio.
