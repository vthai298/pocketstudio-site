Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

This policy explains what information PocketStudio ("we") handles when a channel owner ("you") connects a YouTube channel. PocketStudio uses YouTube API Services. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and use of YouTube is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Information we access

  • Google authorization tokens for the channel you connect, obtained through Google's consent screen (scopes: youtube.upload and youtube). We never receive your Google password.
  • Basic channel information: channel ID and channel title, to confirm the right channel is connected.
  • Information about videos we upload: video IDs, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, privacy status and scheduled publish time.
  • Public statistics of videos we uploaded (such as views), if and when this feature is enabled, to improve future videos.

We do not access your email, contacts, comments, messages, payment information or any videos we did not upload.

How we use it

  • To upload approved videos to your channel as private, set their details and schedule their release.
  • To update your channel description and banner when you ask us to.
  • To measure how our own videos perform, so the studio can make better ones.

We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Tokens and video records are stored only on the studio's own computer or server, in storage that is not public and is never committed to source code. Access is limited to the studio operator.

Retention and deletion

  • When you disconnect a channel, we delete its tokens.
  • Channel statistics obtained from YouTube are refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
  • You can ask us to delete all data we hold about your channel at any time by contacting us; we do so within 7 days.

Revoking access

You can revoke PocketStudio's access at any time from your Google Account security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Children

PocketStudio is used only by channel owners (adults). It does not collect any information from viewers. Videos made for children are always marked "made for kids" on YouTube.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its effective date.
